Globe

A lit, textured Earth built entirely from procedural code and real coastline data --- no satellite photos. Terrain, mountains, deserts and ice caps shade under a moving sun, city lights glow on the night side, and clouds drift above it all, rendered with three.js. Click and drag (or touch) to spin it; let go and it coasts, then settles back into a slow idle turn.
